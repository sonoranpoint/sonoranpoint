Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · Effective [date]

This explains what information Sonoran Point collects, why, and what we do with it. We collect only what we need to run the service, and we don't sell personal information or use it for advertising.

What this covers

This policy covers our website (sonoranpoint.com), the Sonoran Point dashboard, and the stations. "We" means [legal name].

What we collect

When you use the dashboard

  • Your name, email address, company and role.
  • When you sign in, and basic security logs (such as your IP address and browser) so we can keep accounts safe.
  • When someone agrees to our terms for their company: their name, email, the version, the time, their IP address and browser, kept as a record of the agreement.

From the stations

Each station reports whether it's online, its software version, and the network it's connected to, so we can keep it working and updated.

On our website

  • If you use the contact form: your name, email, company, phone number if you give it, and your message.
  • We don't use advertising or tracking cookies. The site remembers whether you chose light or dark mode, in your own browser only. The dashboard stores what it needs to keep you signed in.

Information about crews

Companies that use Sonoran Point add information about their employees and contractors: names, employee numbers, badge IDs and badge barcodes, contract, department, positions and shifts. The stations record each checkout and return: what, when, by whom and at which station.

We handle this information for the company that uses Sonoran Point, and only on its instructions. That company decides what's collected and how it's used, and is responsible for telling its crews. If you're a crew member with a question about your information, please ask your employer first; we'll help them answer you.

How we use it

  • To run the service: sign-in, checkouts and returns, reports, alerts and the dashboard.
  • To send emails the service needs: sign-in links, invites, alerts, trial and billing notices.
  • To support you, keep the service secure, and look into problems or misuse.
  • To improve the service, using statistics that don't identify any company or person.
  • To meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of companies to run the service. They handle information only on our behalf and under contracts that require them to protect it:

  • Supabase: our database and sign-in service.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the website and dashboard and protects them from attacks.
  • Resend: sends our emails.
  • [Payment processor, if you use one]

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or as part of a sale or reorganisation of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply. We don't sell personal information.

Where it's kept

Information is stored in the United States [confirm the Supabase region].

How long we keep it

  • Company and crew information: for as long as the company uses Sonoran Point, then as described in our Terms of Service (deleted within [90] days of the service ending, apart from backups that are overwritten over time).
  • Records of agreements to our terms: for as long as we have a relationship with the company and for [7] years after, as proof.
  • Contact form messages: up to [2] years, unless they become part of a customer account.

Security

Information is encrypted in transit, each company's data is kept separate inside the database, and access is limited by role. Details are on our security page.

Your choices

  • Dashboard users can change their name and password in the dashboard, and ask their company's Admin to change or remove their access.
  • You can ask us for a copy of your information, or to correct or delete it, by emailing support@sonoranpoint.com. If it's information we hold for your employer, we'll pass the request to them.
  • Depending on where you live, the law may give you other rights. Contact us and we'll help.

Children

Sonoran Point is a workplace tool and isn't meant for children. We don't knowingly collect information about anyone under 16.

Changes

We'll update this policy when what we do changes, and change the version and date at the top. If a change matters to how your information is used, we'll tell customers by email or in the dashboard before it takes effect.
