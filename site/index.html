So easy nobody skips it

Tracking only works if people do it every time. A station by the door makes it a three-second habit: no logins, no typing, no clipboard.

  1. Tap your badgeThe station says hello by name and shows what you already have out.
  2. Tap the itemNew items check out to you; items you have come back. Things that already carry a barcode can be scanned instead.
  3. DoneIt asks if anything you returned has a problem, then signs you out on its own.

Accountability without chasing anyone

Every checkout has a name and a time on it. Managers see the whole picture on any computer or phone.

  • Who has it, since whenTap any item at a station, or search the dashboard, and see who has it right now.
  • Late and long-out alertsSet a time limit per item or type. Late items show up straight away, and by email to the people who want them.
  • A report at the end of every shiftWhat went out, what came back and what's still missing, ready to sign off.
  • Problems reported on the spotDamaged, not charging, missing a part. Damaged items are flagged before the next person takes them.
  • The full history, ready to exportEvery checkout and return, kept, and one click from a spreadsheet.
