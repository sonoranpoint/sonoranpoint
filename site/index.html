Three taps at the door

The station sits by the equipment room. Nobody types, nobody signs a clipboard.

  1. Tap your badgeThe station says hello by name and shows what you already have out.
  2. Tap the equipmentNew items check out to you; items you have come back. Gear that already has a barcode can be scanned instead.
  3. DoneIt asks if anything you returned has a problem, then signs you out on its own.

Know what's out before the shift ends

Supervisors get a dashboard on any computer or phone, and only the emails they ask for.

  • Overdue and out-too-long alertsOn the dashboard, and by email to the people who want them.
  • A report at the end of every shiftWhat went out, what came back and what's still missing, ready to sign off.
  • Problems reported at the stationDamaged, not charging, missing a part. Damaged gear is flagged before the next person takes it.
  • Separate contracts that never mixDelta and United below wing each see their own gear and people, with loans between them when you allow it.
  • Download any listActivity, equipment and shift reports go straight to a spreadsheet.

Built for the equipment room

A touchscreen station with an NFC reader built in. Mount it by the door and plug it in.

  • Keeps working when the Wi-Fi dropsTaps are saved on the station and sync when it's back online.
  • Sets up with a codePlug it in, join the Wi-Fi, type the code from the box.
  • Updates itselfNew versions install when nobody is using it, and roll back on their own if something's wrong.
  • Your logo and colorsCrews see your company on the screen, not ours.
  • NFC tags and the barcodes you already haveBadges and gear with existing barcodes work with a handheld scanner, wired or wireless.
  • Try it before you talk to anyoneThe demo station runs in your browser: tap badges, report a problem, open the admin menu.
